# Locked In privacy policy

**Effective:** October 1, 2026
**Developer:** Locked In Labs (lockedinlabs.work)
**Contact:** support@lockedinlabs.work

Locked In is a workout tracker. This policy explains what it stores, where, and how to delete it.

## What the app stores

- **Workouts:** exercises, sets, weights, reps and dates you log.
- **Plans:** your session plans, progression plans and custom exercises.
- **Profile (optional):** name, avatar choice, birth year, height, body weight, goal and experience level.
- **Settings:** theme and rest timer length.

If you sign in with Google, we also receive your Google account's **name, email address and a user id** from
Google, to identify your backup.

The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking, does not read your location or contacts, and does not
sell or share your data.

## Where it's kept

- **On your phone:** everything above is stored on your device. The app works fully without an account.
- **Cloud backup (only if you sign in):** a copy of your workouts, plans, profile and body weight is stored in
  Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), operated by Google on our behalf, so you can restore
  it on another phone. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google. Only your signed-in account can read it.
- **Android backup:** Android may include the app's data in your device's own Google backup, depending on your
  phone settings.

## How long we keep it

Your backup is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your backup and account are deleted
right away.

## Deleting your data

- **In the app:** Profile → Delete account. This deletes your cloud backup and your Locked In account, and can also
  erase the data on your phone.
- **Without the app:** email support@lockedinlabs.work from your Google account's email address and ask us to
  delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days. See lockedinlabs.work/delete-account.
- **Data on your phone only:** uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings.

## Children

Locked In is not directed at children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and the app's Play Store listing.
